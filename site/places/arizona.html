How to take part in a Arizona legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Arizona legislative hearing, register through the legislature's Request to Speak system.

Use the Legislature's Request to Speak (RTS) system: sign in, find the bill on an upcoming agenda, record whether you're for or against it, and ask to speak at the hearing if you want to.

Request to speak ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Arizona hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Arizona's official form for taking part: https://apps.azleg.gov/RequestToSpeak/UpcomingAgendas
  3. Find the agenda item, then record your position or ask to speak.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
