How to take part in a Illinois legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Illinois legislative hearing, file an online witness slip before the hearing starts.

Illinois is the national model: my.ilga.gov lets you record support, opposition, or no position on a bill when a committee hearing is posted.

Official Illinois page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Illinois hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Illinois's official page for taking part: https://my.ilga.gov/
  3. Fill in the slip: who you are, your position (for, against or no position) and whether you want to testify. It closes when the hearing starts, and it is public.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
