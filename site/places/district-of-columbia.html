How to take part in a District of Columbia legislative hearing

To weigh in at a District of Columbia legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

The DC Council posts witness sign-up and written testimony instructions on each hearing notice. That is their version of a slip.

Official District of Columbia page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming District of Columbia hearings are listed below).
  2. Open District of Columbia's official page for taking part: https://dccouncil.gov/events/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Upcoming District of Columbia hearings
