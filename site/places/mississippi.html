How to take part in a Mississippi legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Mississippi legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

Appear in person at the committee meeting, or write to the committee chair. Mississippi has no online comment form.

Official Mississippi legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Mississippi hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Mississippi's official legislature site is https://www.legislature.ms.gov/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
