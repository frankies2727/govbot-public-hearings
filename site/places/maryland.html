How to take part in a Maryland legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Maryland legislative hearing, file an online witness slip before the hearing starts.

Maryland’s MyMGA portal is the other big sibling of Illinois slips: online witness signup and testimony upload for House and Senate hearings.

Official Maryland page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Maryland hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Maryland's official page for taking part: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/
  3. Fill in the slip: who you are, your position (for, against or no position) and whether you want to testify. It closes when the hearing starts, and it is public.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
