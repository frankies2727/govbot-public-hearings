How to take part in a Indiana legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Indiana legislative hearing, fill out the committee's appearance form.

Indiana uses an Appearance Form to speak in committee. It is a request to testify, not a tally of positions like an Illinois slip.

Official Indiana page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Indiana hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Indiana's official page for taking part: https://iga.in.gov/legislative/appearance_form
  3. Fill out the appearance form to speak or to put your position on the record.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Upcoming Indiana hearings
