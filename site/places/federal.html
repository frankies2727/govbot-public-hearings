How to comment on a federal rule

To comment on a federal rule, open it on Regulations.gov and submit your comment before its comment deadline. Comments are public and the agency must consider them.

Find the rule or notice on Regulations.gov and submit a comment before its deadline, or register for an agency's public hearing when one is offered. Comments are public.

Submit a public comment ↗

Steps

  1. Find a proposed rule open for comment (the soonest-closing are listed below).
  2. Open it on Regulations.gov and choose Comment.
  3. Say what you support or oppose and why, with facts from your own experience, then submit before the deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
