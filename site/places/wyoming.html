How to take part in a Wyoming legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Wyoming legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

Wyoming is a small, in-person legislature. There is no e-slip desk; you appear or write the committee.

Official Wyoming page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Wyoming hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Wyoming's official page for taking part: https://www.wyoleg.gov/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Upcoming Wyoming hearings

None are posted right now. Legislatures post hearings a few days to a few weeks ahead, mostly while in session. Follow Wyoming by RSS to hear when one is.
