How to take part in a Rhode Island legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Rhode Island legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Rhode Island posts hearing notices with instructions to submit written testimony to the committee.

Official Rhode Island page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Rhode Island hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Rhode Island's official page for taking part: https://www.rilegislature.gov/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
