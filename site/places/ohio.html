How to take part in a Ohio legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Ohio legislative hearing, file an online witness slip before the hearing starts.

Ohio still uses witness slips — usually attached to the committee notice. House has a standard form; Senate slips vary by committee.

Official Ohio page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Ohio hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Ohio's official page for taking part: https://www.legislature.ohio.gov/
  3. Fill in the slip: who you are, your position (for, against or no position) and whether you want to testify. It closes when the hearing starts, and it is public.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
