How to take part in a Missouri legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Missouri legislative hearing, file an online witness slip before the hearing starts.

Use the House's online Witness Form or the Senate's Witness Registration System to record your position for a hearing.

File a witness slip ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Missouri hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Missouri's official form for taking part: https://www.house.mo.gov/WitnessForm/Default.aspx
  3. Fill in the slip: who you are, your position (for, against or no position) and whether you want to testify. It closes when the hearing starts, and it is public.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
