How to take part in a Alabama legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Alabama legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

Alabama has no statewide witness-slip or e-comment portal. You testify in person or email the committee chair listed on the hearing notice.

Official Alabama page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Alabama hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Alabama's official page for taking part: https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
