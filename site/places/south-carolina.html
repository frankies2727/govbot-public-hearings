How to take part in a South Carolina legislative hearing

To weigh in at a South Carolina legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

South Carolina has no e-slip portal. Appear in committee or write the chair listed on the agenda.

Official South Carolina page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming South Carolina hearings are listed below).
  2. Open South Carolina's official page for taking part: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
