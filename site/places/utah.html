How to take part in a Utah legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Utah legislative hearing, send a public comment through the official form before the deadline.

Submit a public comment on the bill through the Legislature's website during session.

Official Utah legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Utah hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Utah's official legislature site is https://le.utah.gov/
  3. Write your comment, name the bill or topic, and send it through the official form before the deadline.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
