How to take part in a Virginia legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Virginia legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Send written comments to the committee through the committee's page on Virginia's Legislative Information System, around the hearing.

Official Virginia legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Virginia hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Virginia's official legislature site is https://lis.virginia.gov/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
