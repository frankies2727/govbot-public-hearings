How to take part in a Arkansas legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Arkansas legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

Arkansas does not run a public slip portal. Appear in committee or write the chair using the hearing notice.

Official Arkansas page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Arkansas hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Arkansas's official page for taking part: https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
