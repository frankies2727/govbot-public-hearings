How to take part in a Chicago City Council City Council meeting

To weigh in at a Chicago City Council City Council meeting, send a written comment by the deadline in the meeting's notice.

Official Chicago City Council legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Chicago City Council hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Chicago City Council's official legislature site is https://www.chicityclerk.com/about-city-government-chicago-city-council#ChicagoCityCouncilPublicCommentProcess
  3. Write your comment, name the bill or topic, and send it through the official form before the deadline.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
