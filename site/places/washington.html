How to take part in a Washington legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Washington legislative hearing, sign in online for that committee meeting to testify or record your position.

Washington’s Committee Sign-In is the state’s witness slip: register to testify or submit written comments, usually until 24 hours after the hearing starts.

Official Washington page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Washington hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Washington's official page for taking part: https://app.leg.wa.gov/csi/
  3. Sign in for that meeting and choose whether to testify or only note your position.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
