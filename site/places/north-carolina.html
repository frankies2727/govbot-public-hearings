How to take part in a North Carolina legislative hearing

To weigh in at a North Carolina legislative hearing, send a public comment through the official form before the deadline.

Some committees take online public comment and practice varies by chair, so check the committee calendar. You can also write to the chair.

Official North Carolina legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming North Carolina hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; North Carolina's official legislature site is https://www.ncleg.gov/
  3. Write your comment, name the bill or topic, and send it through the official form before the deadline.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
