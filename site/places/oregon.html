How to take part in a Oregon legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Oregon legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Register online as a witness once a public hearing is scheduled; registration usually closes 30 minutes before the hearing starts.

How to testify ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Oregon hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Oregon's official form for taking part: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/citizen_engagement/Pages/How-to-Testify.aspx
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
