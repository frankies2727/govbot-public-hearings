How to take part in a Iowa legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Iowa legislative hearing, send a public comment through the official form before the deadline.

Subcommittee and public-hearing pages take written comments and remarks by Zoom; comments open when that meeting is posted.

Submit a public comment ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Iowa hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Iowa's official form for taking part: https://www.legis.iowa.gov/committees/publicHearings
  3. Write your comment, name the bill or topic, and send it through the official form before the deadline.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
