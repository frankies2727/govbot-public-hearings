How to take part in a Wisconsin legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Wisconsin legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Wisconsin uses hearing registration and written testimony through the legislature’s site when a public hearing is noticed.

Official Wisconsin page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Wisconsin hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Wisconsin's official page for taking part: https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
