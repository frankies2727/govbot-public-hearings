How to take part in a Louisiana legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Louisiana legislative hearing, testify in person or write to the committee chair before the hearing.

Fill out a witness card in the committee room to testify or put your position on the record.

Official Louisiana legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Louisiana hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Louisiana's official legislature site is https://legis.la.gov/
  3. Email the committee chair before the hearing, or go to the hearing and testify in person.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
