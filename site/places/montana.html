How to take part in a Montana legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Montana legislative hearing, send a public comment through the official form before the deadline.

Montana’s Public Participation app covers remote testimony, written comment, and short messages to a committee — their version of a slip.

Official Montana page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Montana hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Montana's official page for taking part: https://participate.legmt.gov/
  3. Write your comment, name the bill or topic, and send it through the official form before the deadline.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
