How to take part in a Minnesota legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Minnesota legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Minnesota House and Senate each post how to testify and how to send written comments when a hearing is scheduled.

Official Minnesota page for taking part ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Minnesota hearings are listed below).
  2. Open Minnesota's official page for taking part: https://www.leg.mn.gov/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.
