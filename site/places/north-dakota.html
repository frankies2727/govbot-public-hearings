How to take part in a North Dakota legislative hearing

To weigh in at a North Dakota legislative hearing, send written testimony to the committee before the hearing.

Submit written testimony for a hearing through the Legislature's website during session, where hearing documents are posted.

Official North Dakota legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming North Dakota hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; North Dakota's official legislature site is https://www.ndlegis.gov/
  3. Write your testimony, name the bill number, and send it to the committee before the hearing.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
