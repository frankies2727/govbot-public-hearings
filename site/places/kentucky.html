How to take part in a Kentucky legislative hearing

To weigh in at a Kentucky legislative hearing, fill out the committee's appearance form.

Fill out the committee's Testimony Request form to ask to speak at a meeting.

Official Kentucky legislature site ↗

Steps

  1. Find the hearing for the bill or topic you care about (upcoming Kentucky hearings are listed below).
  2. Follow the hearing notice; Kentucky's official legislature site is https://legislature.ky.gov/Committees/Pages/default.aspx
  3. Fill out the appearance form to speak or to put your position on the record.
  4. Check the hearing notice for its exact rules and deadline.

Tips

  • Name the bill number (or the rule's docket number) so your input reaches the right item.
  • Say whether you support or oppose it, and why, in a few sentences from your own experience.
  • Send it before the deadline on the hearing notice: many forms close when the hearing starts.
  • What you submit is usually public record, including your name.
